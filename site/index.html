Team Peri Peri Fries · forensic examination protocol engine

Evidence a court can weigh.

This is not a deepfake detector. It takes one video exhibit and returns a base-10 likelihood ratio between two explicitly stated propositions, a declaration of whether the exhibit even falls inside the population it was calibrated on, the exact laundering strength at which its own conclusion breaks, a mandatory abstention path, and a hash-sealed examination record that a second run reproduces byte-for-byte.

Every existing tool outputs P(fake) = 0.97. A court cannot do anything with that number: no stated propositions, no declared population, no validated domain, no uncertainty, no way to reproduce it. The deliverable here is not a better score — it is a verdict that can be cross-examined, including the verdict “inconclusive”.

The two propositions

Stated verbatim in the code, in the API response, and on the report's findings page. Everything the system reports is a ratio between exactly these two, and nothing else.

Hp — prosecution-neutral

The exhibit is an unmanipulated recording of a real event.

Hd — alternative

The exhibit is synthetically generated or materially manipulated in the facial region.

A likelihood ratio answers how much more probable are these findings if Hd is true than if Hp is true — a question about the evidence. It deliberately does not answer is this video fake, which is a question about the world, and which belongs to the Court.

What comes out of one exhibit

Seven outputs, each designed against what a cross-examination would attack.

01Likelihood ratio

A log10 LR between the two propositions above, with the ENFSI verbal equivalent and the supported proposition always named.

02Tamper localisation

Pixel-level mask, reliability map, and a per-frame tamper timeline from an acquisition-consistency network.

03Evidence Fragility Index

An adversarial binary search for the minimum laundering strength at which our own verdict flips.

04Validated-domain check

A Mahalanobis gate answering whether this exhibit is inside the population we calibrated on.

05Mandatory abstention

INCONCLUSIVE with machine-generated reason codes. An instrument that always answers is sometimes wrong without saying so.

06Replayable record

An append-only SHA-256 hash chain of every examination event; a second run yields a byte-identical findings hash.

07Examiner's report

A nine-page PDF structured against India's Section 63(4) BSA Part-B expert declaration, watermarked as a draft for human review.

Attacking our own conclusion

The Evidence Fragility Index binary-searches three independent laundering axes for the minimum strength at which the verdict changes, and reports the answer in court-legible units rather than in model confidence.

The hard rule: the augmentation families used to train the models and the transform families used by the fragility search are disjoint, in both family and parameter range, and the assertion that enforces it runs at import time. If a model were trained on the same degradation we then use to test it, the robustness claim would be circular and would not survive questioning.

Determinism and replay

Reproducibility is a feature of the instrument, not a property of the run. Every float is quantised, every JSON key sorted, every seed fixed and derived by digest rather than by a salted built-in hash. Re-running an examination from its manifest produces the same findings hash, byte for byte — and the dashboard shows the two hashes side by side.

Limitations, stated on the report itself

Page 8 of every generated report carries this text verbatim and unaltered.

Automated detection is probabilistic. Absence of detected manipulation does not establish authenticity. Findings are conditional on the declared validated domain; exhibits outside that domain are reported as inconclusive. This report is forensic decision support prepared to assist an examiner. It does not itself constitute the certificate under Section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, and does not determine admissibility or evidentiary weight, which are matters for the Court.

C2PA precedence: a C2PA manifest verifies that provenance claims have not been tampered with, not that they are truthful. Where forensic findings contradict a manifest, the forensic findings take precedence and both are reported.
